# 🚀 Silly Valley

**Silly Valley** is a retro-style pixel art simulation game where you step into the shoes of a Tech CEO. Build your startup from the ground up—starting in your childhood bedroom or your parents' garage—and scale all the way to a massive Tech Campus in the heart of Silly Valley.

Unlike traditional management sims, your employees are **real AI agents**. Give high-level goals, watch them break tasks down, and manage work visually through an interactive Post-it board.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* 🤖 **AI-Powered Workforce:** Direct autonomous AI agents acting as software engineers, designers, and marketers. They respond to your commands, organize their work, and report progress.
* 📌 **Interactive Post-it Board:** Tasks generated by agents dynamically appear as Post-its on the office wall, giving you a clear, visual overview of your team's workflow and chaos.
* 🏚️ **Garage-to-Unicorn Progression:** 
  * Start small in your childhood bedroom or a cramped garage.
  * Outgrow your space and move into the city of **Silly Valley**.
  * Rent trendy co-working spaces or buy land to custom-build your dream startup headquarters.
* 🎨 **Custom Office Building:** Customize every room, place retro furniture, optimize layout efficiency, and create the ideal environment for your AI team.
* 🕹️ **Nostalgic Pixel Art Aesthetic:** A cozy, detailed pixel-art visual style inspired by classic management and simulation games.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Overview

1. **Define the Vision:** As CEO, assign project goals and directions to your AI agents.
2. **Watch the Magic Happen:** Agents discuss, split jobs into actionable items, and post them onto the wall.
3. **Manage & Scale:** Reinvest revenue, hire new AI talent, decorate your space, and relocate to bigger real estate in Silly Valley.
